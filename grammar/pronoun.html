◑ 代词与限定词

反身代词与物主代词的主宾格混用，是写作扣分点。

4 个考点
01 人称代词（主格/宾格/所有格）A2 主格作主语，宾格作宾语（动词后、介词后），形容词性物主代词 + 名词，名词性物主代词单独用。 4 条易错 · 5 题
主格宾格形容词性物主名词性物主反身
Imemyminemyself
youyouyouryoursyourself
hehimhishishimself
sheherherhersherself
ititits—itself
weusouroursourselves
theythemtheirtheirsthemselves
  • This book is mine, not yours.这本书是我的，不是你的。（名词性物主代词）
  • Give it to him, not me.给他，不是给我。（介词/动词后宾格）
介词后用主格
Between you and I, this is wrong. Between you and me, this is wrong.
介词 between 后接宾格。
its 与 it's 混用
The dog lost it's collar. The dog lost its collar.
its = 它的（所有格）；it's = it is / it has（缩写）。
反身代词误用
Myself and Tom went there. Tom and I went there.
反身代词不能单独作主语；提到自己时把 I 放后面（Tom and I）。
each other 与 themselves 混用
They enjoyed themselves at the party. They enjoyed themselves at the party.（正确）/ They looked at each other.（互相）
themselves = 他们自己；each other = 彼此。

02 反身代词固定搭配B1 enjoy oneself / by oneself / help oneself / teach oneself / lo… 1 条易错 · 3 题
  • Help yourself to some cake.请随便吃点蛋糕。
  • She learned to swim by herself.她自学了游泳。（by oneself = 独自）
enjoy 后加宾格
We enjoyed us at the party. We enjoyed ourselves at the party.
enjoy oneself 是固定搭配，必须用反身代词。

03 关系代词（who/which/that/whose）B1 先行词是人用 who/that；是物用 which/that；表所属用 whose；whom 作宾语（正式）。 3 条易错 · 6 题
  • The man who lives next door is a doctor.住在隔壁的男人是医生。（人 + who 作主语）
  • The book which I bought is interesting.我买的那本书很有意思。（物 + which 作宾语）
  • The girl whose father is a teacher is my friend.父亲是老师的那个女孩是我朋友。（所属 → whose）
关系代词与先行词不一致
The book who I read was good. The book which/that I read was good.
先行词是物（book），关系代词用 which/that，不用 who。
关系代词后有重复宾语
The book which I read it was good. The book which I read was good.
which 已经代替 the book 作宾语，后面不能再出现 it。
whose 用于物时写成 which's
The house which's roof is red. The house whose roof is red.
whose 既可指人也可指物，表示所属，没有 which's 这种形式。
练这个考点（6 题）→
04 不定代词（some/any/no/every + body/thing/where）B1 some 用于肯定句与委婉请求；any 用于否定与疑问；形容词修饰不定代词时要后置。 2 条易错 · 3 题
  • Would you like something to drink?你想喝点什么吗？（委婉请求用 some）
  • I have something important to tell you.我有重要的事要告诉你。（形容词后置）
形容词放在不定代词前
I have important something to tell you. I have something important to tell you.
修饰 -body/-thing/-where 的形容词必须后置。
不定代词谓语用复数
Everybody are here. Everybody is here.
everybody/somebody/nobody/everything 视为单数，谓语用 is/has。
