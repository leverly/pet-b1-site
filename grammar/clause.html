⌘ 从句

宾语从句的语序与"时态呼应"是 PET 阅读 Part 4 的核心考点。

01 宾语从句B1 核心 ① 一律用陈述语序（连接词 + 主语 + 谓语）② 主句过去时，从句一般也退成过去时（客观真理除外）。 4 条避坑 · 8 题

① 一律用陈述语序（连接词 + 主语 + 谓语）② 主句过去时，从句一般也退成过去时（客观真理除外）。

  • I don't know where he lives.我不知道他住哪儿。（不是 where does he live）
  • She said that she was tired.她说她累了。（主句过去 → 从句过去）
  • The teacher told us that the earth goes around the sun.老师告诉我们地球绕着太阳转。（客观真理用现在时）
宾语从句用疑问语序（第一大坑）
Do you know where does he live? Do you know where he lives?
宾语从句必须是陈述语序：连接词 + 主语 + 谓语。
主句过去时时态不呼应
He said he will come. He said he would come.
主句是过去时，从句要退时：will→would, can→could, is→was。
if/whether 后加 or not 位置错
I don't know if or not he will come. I don't know whether or not he will come. / whether he will come or not.
if 不能直接跟 or not；whether 可以（whether or not / whether…or not）。
that 从句与疑问词从句混用
I don't know that he is coming. I don't know whether he is coming.
"是否"用 whether/if，不用 that。

02 状语从句（时间/条件/原因/让步/目的/结果）B1 核心 时间：when/while/as/until/before/after/as soon as；条件：if/unless；原因：bec… 5 条避坑 · 9 题

时间：when/while/as/until/before/after/as soon as；条件：if/unless；原因：because/since/as；让步：although/though/even though；目的：so that/in order to；结果：so…that/such…that。

  • Although it was raining, we went out.虽然下雨，我们还是出门了。
  • I'll wait until you come back.我会等到你回来。
  • He ran fast so that he could catch the bus.他跑得很快，为的是能赶上公交。
although 与 but 同时用
Although it was late, but we continued. Although it was late, we continued.
although/though 不能与 but 连用（中文"虽然…但是"的负迁移）。
because 与 so 同时用
Because he was ill, so he stayed home. Because he was ill, he stayed home.
because 与 so 不能同时出现在一个句子里。
unless 后加 not
Unless you don't study, you will fail. Unless you study, you will fail.
unless = if…not，本身已含否定，不能再加 not。
until 与 not…until 混用
He left until the film ended. He didn't leave until the film ended.
until 表持续到某时；"直到…才"用 not…until。
时间/条件从句用将来时
I will tell him when he will come. I will tell him when he comes.
主将从现：时间/条件从句用现在时表将来。

03 定语从句（限制性/非限制性）B1+ 冲卓越 限制性从句 + that/which/who；非限制性从句只能用 which/who（逗号后），不能用 that。 2 条避坑 · 2 题

限制性从句 + that/which/who；非限制性从句只能用 which/who（逗号后），不能用 that。

  • The man who called you is my uncle.给你打电话的那个人是我叔叔。
  • Beijing, which is the capital of China, is very big.北京是中国首都，很大。（非限制性，不能用 that）
非限制性从句用 that
Beijing, that is the capital, is big. Beijing, which is the capital, is big.
逗号隔开的非限制性定语从句不能用 that。
which 与 where 混用
This is the house which I live. This is the house where I live.
从句缺状语（live 后需地点）用 where；缺主语/宾语才用 which/that。
