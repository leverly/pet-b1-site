≈ 形容词与副词

比较级的不规则变化与 than 的搭配，是 PET 语法题稳定得分点。

3 个考点
01 比较级与最高级A2 单音节 + -er/-est；三音节及以上 + more/most；不规则：good-better-best, bad-wo… 5 条易错 · 8 题
原级比较级最高级
bigbiggerbiggest
easyeasiereasiest
beautifulmore beautifulmost beautiful
goodbetterbest
badworseworst
farfurther/fartherfurthest/farthest
littlelessleast
  • She is taller than her sister.她比她姐姐高。
  • This is the most interesting book I have read.这是我读过的最有趣的书。
比较级重复（more + -er）
This is more easier. This is easier.
已经加了 -er 就不能再加 more，二者择一。
比较级缺 than
He is taller his brother. He is taller than his brother.
比较级要有比较对象，用 than 连接。
as…as 中间用比较级
She is as taller as me. She is as tall as me.
as + 原级 + as，中间用原级不是比较级。
最高级漏 the
He is tallest in the class. He is the tallest in the class.
最高级前一般要加 the。
比较对象不对等
My bike is faster than Tom. My bike is faster than Tom's.
自行车要和自行车比，用 Tom's 代替 Tom's bike。
练这个考点（8 题）→
02 形容词变副词 / 形副同形B1 一般 + -ly；以 y 结尾变 i + ly；hard/high/late/fast/early 形副同形或易混。 4 条易错 · 7 题
  • He works hard.他工作努力。（hard 是副词）
  • He hardly works.他几乎不工作。（hardly = 几乎不）
  • She arrived late.她迟到了。（late 形副同形）
  • She has been working lately.她最近一直在工作。（lately = 最近）
hard 与 hardly 混用
He studies hardly. He studies hard.
hard = 努力地；hardly = 几乎不。加了 -ly 意思完全变了。
late 与 lately 混用
He came lately. He came late.
late = 晚/迟；lately = 最近。
系动词后用副词
The soup tastes well. The soup tastes good.
taste/smell/look/sound/feel 是系动词，后接形容词。
形容词修饰动词
She sings beautiful. She sings beautifully.
修饰动词要用副词。
练这个考点（7 题）→
03 enough / too / so…that 结构B1 adj/adv + enough（enough 后置）；too + adj/adv；so + adj/adv + that；… 3 条易错 · 5 题
  • He is old enough to drive.他年龄够大，可以开车了。
  • It's too cold to go out.天太冷了，出不去。
  • It was so cold that we stayed in.天太冷了，我们待在家里。
  • It was such a cold day that we stayed in.那天太冷了，我们待在家里。
enough 位置放错
He is enough old to drive. He is old enough to drive.
enough 修饰形容词/副词时要后置。
so 与 such 混用
It was so a cold day. It was such a cold day.
so + adj/adv；such + (a/an) + adj + 名词。有名词就用 such。
too…to 后加 not
He is too young not to drive. He is too young to drive.
too…to 本身已含否定意义（太…以至于不能），不需要再加 not。
