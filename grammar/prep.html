01 时间介词 in / on / atA2 巩固 at + 时刻/具体点；on + 具体某天/日期；in + 月份/年份/季节/较长时段。 3 条避坑 · 5 题

at + 时刻/具体点；on + 具体某天/日期；in + 月份/年份/季节/较长时段。

介词用于例
at时刻、中午、夜晚、周末（英式）at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night, at the weekend
on星期、日期、具体某天的上下午on Monday, on 5 May, on Friday morning
in月份、年份、季节、上午/下午/晚上、较长时段in May, in 2020, in summer, in the morning
  • I was born in 2008.我 2008 年出生。
  • The meeting is on Monday morning.会议在周一上午。
具体某天用 in
I was born in 5 May. I was born on 5 May.
具体到某一天（日期、星期）用 on。
上午/下午漏 the
in morning in the morning
in the morning/afternoon/evening 都要加 the，但 at night 不加。
in + 时间段表"之后"
I will be back after two weeks. I will be back in two weeks.
in + 时间段 = 在（多久）之后（将来）；after + 时间段也可，但 in 更常用。

02 地点介词 in / on / at / toB1 核心 in 大地点/内部；on 表面；at 具体点；to 表方向（go to）。 3 条避坑 · 4 题

in 大地点/内部；on 表面；at 具体点；to 表方向（go to）。

  • He lives in London.他住在伦敦。（城市用 in）
  • The book is on the table.书在桌上。（表面用 on）
  • I'll meet you at the station.我在车站见你。（具体点用 at）
arrive in/at 选错
We arrived to the airport. We arrived at the airport.
arrive in + 大地点（城市/国家）；arrive at + 小地点。arrive 后不用 to。
go home 加 to
I went to home. I went home.
home 作副词时不加 to；但 go to my home 可以。
on/in the street
I met him in the street.（想表达"在街上"） I met him on the street. / in the street（英式）
on the street（美式）/ in the street（英式）都可，但 on the corner 是固定搭配。

03 高频固定搭配B1 核心 动词/形容词 + 固定介词，必须整体记忆。 3 条避坑 · 8 题

动词/形容词 + 固定介词，必须整体记忆。

搭配含义
depend on依靠；取决于
look forward to期待（to 是介词）
be good at擅长
be interested in对…感兴趣
be afraid of害怕
be proud of为…自豪
be famous for以…闻名
be responsible for对…负责
apologise for为…道歉
congratulate sb on祝贺某人
spend money on在…上花钱
prevent sb from doing阻止某人做
succeed in成功做某事
be used to doing习惯于
instead of代替；而不是
in spite of尽管
because of因为
according to根据
  • I'm interested in music.我对音乐感兴趣。
  • He succeeded in passing the exam.他成功通过了考试。
interested 后用 to
I am interested to music. I am interested in music.
be interested in 是固定搭配。
good at 后用动词原形
She is good at sing. She is good at singing.
介词 at 后接动名词。
because of 与 because 混用
Because of it rained, we stayed in. Because it rained, we stayed in.
because + 句子；because of + 名词/短语。
