01 并列连词A2 巩固 and 并列/递进；but 转折；or 选择；so 结果；for 原因（正式）；yet 然而。 1 条避坑 · 3 题

and 并列/递进；but 转折；or 选择；so 结果；for 原因（正式）；yet 然而。

  • She was tired, so she went to bed early.她累了，所以早睡了。
  • Hurry up, or you will be late.快点，否则你会迟到。
逗号连接两个句子
I was tired, I went to bed. I was tired, so I went to bed. / I was tired. I went to bed.
两个完整句子不能只用逗号连接（逗号粘连），要有连词或句号。

02 连接副词（however / therefore / besides / otherwise）B1 核心 however 然而；therefore 因此；besides 而且；otherwise 否则；moreover 此外；in add… 3 条避坑 · 6 题

however 然而；therefore 因此；besides 而且；otherwise 否则；moreover 此外；in addition 另外。它们是副词，后面接逗号，前面用分号或句号。

  • It was raining; however, we went out.下雨了；然而我们还是出门了。
  • He didn't study. Therefore, he failed.他没学习。因此他不及格。
however 当连词用
It was raining, however we went out. It was raining; however, we went out.
however 是副词，不能像 but 那样直接连接两个句子，要用分号或句号。
but 与 however 重复
But however, I like it. However, I like it. / But I like it.
两个转折词不要叠用。
although 与 however 重复
Although it rained, however we went out. Although it rained, we went out.
although 与 however 不要同时使用。

转述时：时态后退一步、人称转换、时间/地点状语转换（now→then, today→that day, tomorrow→the next day, here→there）。

say to sbtell sb
  • "I am tired," she said. → She said that she was tired.她说她累了。
  • "Will you come?" he asked. → He asked if I would come.他问我是否会来。
转述疑问句仍用疑问语序
He asked me where did I live. He asked me where I lived.
间接引语中的疑问句改为陈述语序。
say 后接人用 to
She said me that she was busy. She told me that she was busy.
say 后接人要用 say to sb；tell 直接接人（tell sb）。
时间状语不转换
She said she would come tomorrow. She said she would come the next day.
转述过去的话，tomorrow 要变 the next day。
