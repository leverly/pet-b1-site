▤ 名词与冠词

可数/不可数与 a/an/the 的取舍，是中国学生写作里最高频的错误。

3 个考点
01 可数与不可数名词A2 可数名词有单复数，单数前要加 a/an/the；不可数名词没有复数，不能加 a/an，用 much/little/a pie… 4 条易错 · 9 题
  • I need some information.我需要一些信息。（information 不可数）
  • She gave me two pieces of advice.她给了我两条建议。（advice 不可数，用 piece 计量）
不可数名词加 -s
I have many informations / advices / furnitures. I have much information / a lot of advice / some furniture.
information/advice/furniture/news/equipment/luggage/money 都是不可数名词，无复数形式。
不可数名词前加 a/an
She gave me an advice. She gave me a piece of advice.
不可数名词不能直接加 a/an，要借助量词。
news 误当复数
The news are good. The news is good.
news 形式像复数，实际是不可数名词，谓语用单数。
people 与 person
Three persons came.（口语中想表达三个人） Three people came.
people 本身是复数（= persons），一个人是 a person。
练这个考点（9 题）→
02 冠词 a / an / the / 零冠词B1 a/an = 泛指一个（首次提到）；the = 特指（双方都知道/独一无二/最高级/序数词）；零冠词 = 泛指复数/不可数、… 5 条易错 · 12 题
  • I saw a dog. The dog was black.我看到一只狗。那只狗是黑色的。（首次 a，再提 the）
  • She plays the piano, but she plays football.她弹钢琴，但踢足球。（乐器加 the，球类不加）
  • He is the tallest boy in class.他是班里最高的男孩。（最高级加 the）
a 与 an 按字母而非音素选
an university / a hour / an European a university / an hour / a European
an 用于元音音素前：hour 的 h 不发音（an hour），university 读 /juː/ 是辅音开头（a university）。
乐器与球类冠词搞反
She plays piano and the football. She plays the piano and football.
演奏乐器 + the；球类运动/棋类 + 零冠词。
三餐/学科加 the
I have the breakfast at 7. / I like the history. I have breakfast at 7. / I like history.
三餐、学科、语言、季节前一般不加冠词。
独一无二事物漏 the
Sun rises in east. The sun rises in the east.
the sun/the moon/the earth/the sky、方位词（the east）都要加 the。
固定搭配冠词错误
go to school by the bus / in the fact go to school by bus / in fact
by + 交通工具（零冠词）、固定短语 in fact / at home / on foot 都不加冠词。
练这个考点（12 题）→
03 数量词（some/any/much/many/few/little）B1 many/few + 可数复数；much/little + 不可数；a few/a little = 有一点（肯定）；few… 3 条易错 · 7 题
可数复数不可数
很多many / a lot ofmuch / a lot of
有一点（肯定）a fewa little
几乎没有（否定）fewlittle
每个/每个every / each—
  • There are a few apples left.还剩几个苹果。（还有一些）
  • There is little water left.几乎没水了。（不够了）
  • How much money do you have?你有多少钱？（money 不可数）
few 与 a few 混用
There are few apples — let's eat some.（想表达"有几个"） There are a few apples — let's eat some.
a few = 有一些（肯定）；few = 几乎没有（否定）。少一个 a，意思反转。
much/many 选错
How many money / how much books How much money / how many books
money 不可数用 much，books 可数用 many。
every 后接复数
Every students have a book. Every student has a book.
every/each + 单数名词 + 单数谓语。
